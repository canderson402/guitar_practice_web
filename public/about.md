# Hello Fellow Learner

I originally made this site to have a custom metronome tool conveniently available. It has since expanded to much more than a metronome. Music theory became a passion of mine during the process of making this, so I expanded it into a general music learning tool.
  
While this was made with guitar in mind you can swap between a fretboard and piano roll to study with.  Have fun and let me know if you have any feedback!

## I LOVE MY CARDS

This was created with what I call my precious card system in mind.  Each card has standalone functionality and covers something specific.  They can then be used seamlessly together.

Workspaces can be used to save a collection of cards, use it however you want.

## Intervals

I made a point to have references to intervals all over the place when they are relevant.  An interval first approach was a huge boon for me personally on this journey.

We don't do CAGED here. We go right to the source.

## The Cards

This is not an exhaustive list of features, but I will try to explain the basics of each card.

### Metronome
- It's a metronome, oh yeah you heard that right baby
- Tempo, Subdivisions, Time Signatures

### Timer
- Simple timer for general use

### Scale
- Can select a scale and see the notes that lay within
- The selected scale is set sitewide, some cards are changed to stay diatonic with it

### Circle of fifths
- Circle of fifths reference
- You can toggle relative mode to see diatonic chords or relative keys

### Note Trainer
- This can advance with the metronome so how I used it originally was to have it advance after  12 beats and I would play one note on each string under the twelfth fret up and down and then I have one beat leftover to advance to the next one.  It's how I learned all the notes on the fretboard
- This can follow the circle of fifths or the cycle of fourths, I use this to practice a scale or phrase in all twelve keys

### Chords
- **In key:** Shows diatonic chords 
- **Any chord:** tool to examine chords
- Selected intervals will be displayed on the fretboard/piano roll card

### Fretboard
- Shows selected things on the fretboard, scales, chord positions, etc...
- Has a bunch of things you can toggle, note names/intervals, show the root, show positions for certain scales...
- Can toggle between a piano roll and guitar fretboard

### Jam
- Tries to generate a backing track in key.   

### Harmony
- This one is a bit experimental, the idea is you can select a melody line, an interval then generate the harmony, you can drag notes around to see different voicings. 

## More Cool Stuff

### Utility
- All cards have a gear button, it opens a panel that allows for deeper controls

### Across the app
- I try to keep the metronome and key accessible at all times and between cards that should share it.
- There is a gear top right of the site, you can change things like the guitar tuning or even the Hz the tuning is based on.

### Sight Reading
- This is really experimental, not sure I love it.  If you do let me know.
